### Power Consumption 
1. Dynamic Power Consumption
   1. power used to charge capacitance as signal change.   
   2. = C * V^2 *f
2. Static Power Consumption
   1. Power used when the signal do not change.
   2. v * I (leaked current)
3. Energy Consumption = power * time
